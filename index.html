About the Digital Safety & Literacy Simulator

This software project is developed as a college Societal Impact Project under the theme "Education, Digital Literacy and Skill Development." With India and the world rapidly digitizing everyday services, cyber fraud has become a pressing societal challenge that disproportionately affects students, senior citizens, and first-time smartphone users.

Project Category Education & Digital Literacy
Implementation Model 100% Software-Only Simulation
Target Audience College Students, Youth & First-time Internet Users
National Helpline Reference 1930 (MHA Cyber Helpline) / cybercrime.gov.in

Core Societal Objectives

  • Experiential Learning: Rather than boring lectures, users learn through realistic hands-on interaction without risking real money or data.
  • Preventing UPI & Payment Fraud: Dispelling the pervasive myth that entering a UPI PIN is required to receive money or refunds.
  • Zero-Trust Credential Security: Teaching strict vigilance against sharing OTPs, clicking suspicious attachments (.exe/.docm), or using cloned social profiles.
  • Civic Awareness & Helplines: Educating users on the "Golden Hour" of reporting financial cyber fraud via national helpline 1930.
Important Software-Only Certification: This application does not require any specialized hardware or access to real SMS inboxes, WhatsApp conversations, banking portals, or personal contacts. All data is static, local, and simulated for educational safety.
